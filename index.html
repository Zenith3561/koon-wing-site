How we work

From sketch to packed carton.

由概念到出貨 · 五個階段

STEP 01

Brief & concept

接洽與概念

You send a sketch, a reference sample or just a budget and a deadline. We come back with formats that fit.

STEP 02

Design & artwork

設計與圖稿

Artwork is drawn up to production spec — line weights, enamel separations, plating and engraving areas.

STEP 03

Tooling & sample

開模與打樣

Tooling is cut and a physical pre-production sample is sent for your written sign-off before anything runs.

STEP 04

Production & QC

生產與品檢

The approved sample is the reference. Goods are inspected against it before they leave the factory.

STEP 05

Packing & delivery

包裝與交付

Boxed, cartoned and shipped to Hong Kong or direct to your market, with the paperwork your side needs.
